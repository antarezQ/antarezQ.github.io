Classroom Occupancy Analysis

PythonpandasSQLStreamlitPower BI
QuestionThe building is being renovated down to 32 classrooms. Are rooms really full, and how should faculties share them?
ApproachCleaned the real timetable (290 weekly classes), measured use per room every 30 minutes, then built a faculty room plan that keeps every class time as it is.
OutcomeRooms were used 42% of the time on average. Every class fits the plan, and reserve rooms stay free even at the busiest hour.
